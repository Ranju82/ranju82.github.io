Letter Merge – Privacy Policy

App: Letter Merge (com.rtech.lettermerge) · Developer: RoshniTech · Last updated: 11 November 2026

Letter Merge is a word puzzle game. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels completed, stars, scores, daily streak, hints), your settings (theme, sound, vibration, reminder) and whether you bought "Remove Ads" are saved only on your device. Android may include this data in your personal device backup. We cannot see it.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google and its advertising partners:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID
  • App interaction data, such as ad views and taps
  • Diagnostic data, such as crash logs and performance information
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address

In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and some US states, the app asks for your consent or choices through Google's consent form before personalised ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Android settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

"Remove Ads" is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your card or payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether the purchase is active.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily puzzle reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No data is sent to us for this.

Children

Letter Merge is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

Because we don't keep any data about you on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes all game data from your device.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
