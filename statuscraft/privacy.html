StatusCraft Privacy Policy

Developer: RoshniTech · App: StatusCraft (com.rtech.statuscraft) · Effective: 28 October 2026

StatusCraft helps you make and share quotes, Good Morning wishes, festival greetings and status images in many Indian languages. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. We have kept the app simple: there is no sign-up, no account and no StatusCraft server that stores your data.

1. Information that stays on your phone

These settings are saved only on your device so the app works the way you set it up. We do not receive them.

  • Your chosen content languages, theme and reminder time
  • Quotes you save as favourites
  • The name you choose to add to your cards (for example "With love, Priya")
  • Simple counters, such as how many times you have shared, used to show tips at the right moment

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include these settings in your device backup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by us.

2. Images and text you share

Cards you create are made on your phone. When you tap Share, the image or text goes to the app you pick (such as WhatsApp). When you tap Save, the image is saved to your phone's gallery. We do not receive or store your cards. If you leave "Add app link when sharing" on, a short caption with a link to StatusCraft on Google Play is added; you can turn this off in Settings.

3. Notifications

Daily wish reminders and festival alerts are scheduled on your phone. They are not sent from our servers and no data is collected to send them. You can turn them off in Settings or in Android settings.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

StatusCraft is free and shows ads using Google AdMob, which is provided by Google. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, including your Android advertising ID
  • Your IP address, which can indicate approximate location
  • Information about how you interact with ads and the app
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as crash logs

Google uses this information under its own policies. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices:

  • If you are in the UK, the European Economic Area or another region where consent is required, the app asks for your consent before ads are requested. You can change your choice any time in StatusCraft Settings under "Ad privacy choices".
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads, depending on your phone).

We ask Google to show only ads suitable for general audiences and block sensitive categories.

6. Children

StatusCraft is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us.

7. Deleting your data

Because your settings are stored only on your phone, you can delete them at any time by clearing the app's storage (Android Settings → Apps → StatusCraft → Storage → Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app. To manage data Google holds, such as advertising data, use your Google account controls or your phone's ad settings described above.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app uses information, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes on Google Play.
