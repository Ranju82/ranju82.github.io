EasyPrint

Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026 · App ID com.rtech.easyprint

EasyPrint prints your files directly from your phone to your printer. Your documents go to your printer and nowhere else. We do not collect, store or sell your personal data. The free app shows banner ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described in section 6.

At a glance

Do we collect personal data?No
Do your documents leave your phone?Only to the printer you choose
Do you need an account?No
Advertising?Yes, banner ads from Google AdMob (section 6)
Analytics or crash reporting?None
Does any third party receive data?Only Google AdMob, to show ads. It never sees your documents or printers

1Your documents

When you open a PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, image or text file, EasyPrint reads it on your phone to show a preview and to build a print-ready file. Office files are converted to PDF on your phone.

When you print, the file is sent straight to the printer you picked, over your local network (Wi-Fi or Ethernet) or a USB cable. It is never uploaded to us or to any cloud service.

Temporary print files are kept in the app's private cache on your phone. Android can clear them at any time, and they are removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

2Printers and settings

The app stores the printers you add (name, network address or USB identifiers), your default printer and your preferences, such as theme and paper size. This information stays on your phone in the app's private storage. We never receive it.

If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include these settings in your own backup, such as your Google account backup. That backup is controlled by you and your backup provider, not by us.

3Permissions the app uses

  • Network access lets the app send print jobs to network printers and check their status.
  • Wi-Fi multicast lets the app find printers on your local network automatically.
  • USB access lets the app print to a printer connected by cable. Android asks for your permission for each printer.
  • Notifications tell you when downloaded printer support is ready, or when a download failed (see section 5). You can turn them off in Android settings.
  • Advertising ID lets Google AdMob show and measure ads (see section 6). You can reset or delete it in Android settings.

Files are opened only through the Android file picker, so the app can read only the files you choose.

4Printer support reports

If your printer does not work, you can tap My printer isn't supported. This creates a short text report with the printer's model and the printer languages it reports. It contains no documents and no personal details.

You see the report before anything is sent, and nothing is sent unless you choose to. You can share it through the Android share menu with the app you pick, such as your email app, or open a pre-filled issue on our public GitHub issue tracker in your browser. A GitHub issue is public and is posted under your own GitHub account, and GitHub's privacy policy applies to it. We use these reports only to add support for more printers.

5Printer support downloads

The app may offer to download extra printer support (small data files that tell EasyPrint how to talk to a printer). They contain no program code.

  • Catalog check. About once a week the app downloads the list of available printer support, so it can offer support for your printer when you need it. This happens in the background, on Wi-Fi only unless you change that in Settings.
  • Downloads. Printer support is downloaded only after you agree, or automatically if you choose Always in Settings.
  • Your control. Choose Never in EasyPrint › Settings to stop both the catalog check and downloads.

Like any internet request, these requests reveal your device's IP address to the server. Nothing else is sent: no account, documents, printer list or printer details. The server is used only to deliver these files.

6Advertising

EasyPrint is free and shows a small banner ad from Google AdMob on its main screens. Ads never appear on the print preview, and the ad service never receives your documents, file names, printers or print settings.

To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects the following and sends it to Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location may be worked out
  • device and app information, such as phone model, Android version, language and app version
  • ad interactions, such as ads shown and tapped, and diagnostic data about ad performance

This data is encrypted in transit. Google processes it under its own policies: see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your consent. Where the law requires it, for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in EasyPrint › Settings › Ad privacy choices. If you decline, you may still see non-personalised ads, which are based on things like the app and your approximate location.

Your advertising ID. You can reset or delete it in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads (on some phones Settings › Google › Ads).

7What we don't do

  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We don't track your location or build a profile of you ourselves.
  • We don't sell or rent personal data. Apart from what Google AdMob collects to show ads (section 6), we don't share it with anyone.

8Children

EasyPrint is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not collect personal data from anyone. Ads in the app come from Google AdMob, as described in section 6.

9Your choices

The app keeps your printers, settings and files on your phone, so you control them. You can delete saved printers in the app, or remove everything by going to Settings › Apps › EasyPrint › Storage › Clear data or uninstalling the app. To change your ad choices or your advertising ID, see section 6.

10Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change affects what data the app handles, we will mention it in the app's release notes.
