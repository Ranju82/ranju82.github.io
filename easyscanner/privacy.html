Privacy Policy: Easy Scanner

Effective date: 10 November 2026 · Developer: RTech · Contact: ranjusingha81@gmail.com

Summary. Easy Scanner works on your phone. Your documents, scans, signatures and the text recognized in them stay on your device. The app has no account system and no analytics, and we run no servers that receive your data. The home screen shows a banner ad from Google AdMob; see Advertising below for what that involves.

Downloads from Google

The app uses Google Play services and Google ML Kit. To work, they may download models: text recognition for some scripts, translation languages, and Gemini Nano. Google may also collect limited diagnostic and usage information about these components (such as device model, OS version, app version, and error and performance data), as described in Google's ML Kit data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy. RTech does not receive this information.

Advertising

The home screen shows a banner ad served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects information from your device such as your IP address, the device's advertising ID, device and app information, approximate location derived from the IP address, how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data. Google may use it to personalize ads, unless you opt out. This is handled by Google under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Advertising. RTech does not receive this data in a form that identifies you. Your documents, scans and their text are never shared with the ad SDK.

  • Your choices: in the EEA, the UK and other regions where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before showing ads, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy → Ads).

When you contact us

If you email us, including when you use Contact support or Report on an AI answer, the app opens your own email app with a message you can read and edit before sending. Nothing is sent unless you send it. We then receive your email address and whatever the message contains (for a report: the AI response you chose to quote, the app version and your phone model). We use it only to reply to you and to improve the app, never share it, and delete it on request.

Sharing

The app shares a document only when you choose to: through the share menu, the print dialog, or a backup you save. The app you share with handles the document under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Internet: used to download the models described above and to load ads.
  • Advertising ID: requested by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show and measure ads (see Advertising).
  • Camera: the app doesn't request camera permission. Scanning uses Google's document scanner (part of Google Play services), which opens the camera only while you scan.
  • Photos and files: the app reads only the photos and files you pick in the system picker (Images → PDF, importing PDFs, a signature from a photo).

Data retention and deletion

Documents stay on your phone until you delete them in the app. Uninstalling the app deletes everything in its private storage. Backup files you saved elsewhere are not affected. To have emails you sent us deleted, write to ranjusingha81@gmail.com. Data collected by Google for ads is kept and deleted under Google's policies; you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time as described above.

Security

Documents are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read. You can also protect individual PDFs with a password (AES-256 encryption).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children, and ads in the app are not intended for children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at this address.
