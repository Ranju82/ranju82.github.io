BluChat

Privacy Policy

Developer: RoshniTech · App: BluChat (com.rtech.bluchat) · Effective 21 October 2026

In short: BluChat does not collect, store on any server, or share any of your personal data. Messages, photos, videos and files travel directly between two phones over Bluetooth and are saved only on those phones. BluChat has no servers, no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

1. Information we collect

We (RoshniTech, the developer of BluChat) do not collect any information about you. BluChat does not connect to the internet or to any server, so nothing you do in the app is ever sent to us or to anyone else except the person you choose to chat with.

2. Information stored on your phone

To work as a chat app, BluChat keeps the following on your own device, in storage that only BluChat can access:

  • The messages you send and receive, with the time of each message.
  • Copies of photos, videos, voice messages and files you send or receive in a chat.
  • Names and photos you choose for the people you chat with. These are never sent to anyone.
  • The Bluetooth name and address of the phones you chat with, so each conversation can be shown again later.
  • Simple app settings, such as whether you have seen the welcome screen.

This information is not uploaded or backed up to the cloud by BluChat, and Android's automatic app backup is turned off for BluChat.

3. How information is shared

When you chat, your messages and the photos, videos or files you choose to send go directly to the other phone over a Bluetooth connection. They are not shared with us or with any third party. Custom names and photos you set for contacts stay on your phone and are never sent. BluChat uses Android's secure Bluetooth connection, which requires the two phones to be paired and encrypts the link between them.

4. Permissions the app uses

Nearby devices / BluetoothTo find phones nearby, connect to them, send and receive messages and files, and (only when you tap “Make this phone visible”) let other phones find yours.
Location (Android 11 and older only)Older versions of Android require location permission to scan for Bluetooth devices. BluChat never reads, stores or shares your location.
Photos, videos and filesOnly the items you pick in Android's photo or file picker are read, so they can be sent in a chat or used as a contact photo. BluChat cannot see any other files on your phone.
MicrophoneUsed only while you hold the mic button to record a voice message. The recording is sent directly to the person you are chatting with and kept in that chat on your phone.

5. Third-party services

BluChat does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services. The open-source libraries it uses (such as Android Jetpack libraries and the Coil image library) run only on your device and do not send data anywhere.

When you choose to open a received photo, video or file, Android passes only that item to the app you pick (for example a gallery or document viewer). That app's own privacy policy then applies to it.

6. Deleting your data

  • To delete one conversation, open the chat, tap the menu and choose Clear chat. This removes its messages, photos and files from your phone.
  • To delete a single message, photo, voice message or file, long-press it and choose Delete. It is removed from your phone only.
  • To remove a contact's custom name or photo, long-press the contact, then clear the name or choose Remove photo.
  • To delete everything, uninstall BluChat or clear its storage in Android Settings.
  • Messages or files already delivered to another phone are stored on that phone and can only be deleted by its owner.

7. Children

BluChat is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes on Google Play.
