Privacy Policy: Meitei Mayek

Effective date: 21 November 2026 · Developer: RTech · Contact: ranjusingha81@gmail.com

Summary. Meitei Mayek teaches the Meitei Mayek script. Your learning progress stays on your phone. The app has no account, no sign-up and no analytics, and we run no servers that receive your data. The main screens show a banner ad from Google AdMob (never during lessons); see Advertising below for what that involves.

Advertising

The Learn, Alphabet, Practice and Profile screens show a banner ad served by Google AdMob. No ads are shown during lessons. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects information from your device such as your IP address, the device's advertising ID, device and app information, approximate location derived from the IP address, how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data. Google may use it to personalize ads, unless you opt out. This is handled by Google under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Advertising. RTech does not receive this data in a form that identifies you. Your learning progress is never shared with the ad SDK.

  • Your choices: in the EEA, the UK and other regions where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before showing ads, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy → Ads).

Text-to-speech

When no recording is bundled for a sound, the app uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone. The app sends only the letter or word to be spoken to that engine, on your device, and nothing about you.

When you contact us

If you use Report a mistake or email us, the app opens your own email app with a message you can read and edit before sending. Nothing is sent unless you send it. We then receive your email address and whatever the message contains (for a report: the letter, word or lesson you are reporting). We use it only to reply to you and to fix the course, never share it, and delete it on request.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used only to load ads.
  • Advertising ID: requested by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show and measure ads (see Advertising).
  • The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Android backup

If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include the app's progress and settings in your own Google backup, so they can be restored on a new phone. That backup is controlled by Google and your account settings, not by us.

Data retention and deletion

Your progress stays on your phone until you use Settings → Reset all progress, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. To have emails you sent us deleted, write to ranjusingha81@gmail.com. Data collected by Google for ads is kept and deleted under Google's policies; you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time as described above.

Children

The app is a general-audience language course and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children, and ads in the app are not intended for children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at this address.
